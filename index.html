Assam Association Vancouver (Aavan)

Who We Are

Assam Association of Vancouver is a community-driven non-profit organization devoted to preserving Assamese heritage and celebrating cultural identity in British Columbia.

Through festivals, cultural programs, and shared experiences, we create opportunities for families, youth, and community members to stay connected to their roots while building lasting friendships in Canada.

We are proud to foster a spirit of inclusivity, multicultural harmony, and community support by bringing people together through culture, tradition, and belonging.

Our Mission

The Assam Association of Vancouver, founded in 2017, is a non-profit, socio-cultural organization to promote, foster, and showcase the rich cultural heritage of Assam in India. It primarily promotes multiculturalism and racial tolerance among the different communities originating from Assam as well as from different parts of the world in the province of British Columbia.

We focus on helping the younger generation preserve, protect, and advance our cultural heritage. We promote our culture to the broader Canadian community by organizing our cultural events and participating in other cultural events. We assist people in British Columbia originating from or with ties to Assam, India, in their economic or professional pursuits.

The association is also engaged in helping the local community with charitable work like food banks and disaster relief. We provide volunteering opportunities to our community youth, as well as bursaries and awards to youths/students who showcase exemplary commitment to cultural and community involvement.
