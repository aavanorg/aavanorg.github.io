ABOUT AAVAN

Culture, connection and belonging.

We bring Assamese families, students, newcomers and friends together through cultural celebrations, community support and shared experiences across British Columbia.

Culture

Celebrate Assamese traditions, music, food, festivals and the heritage that connects generations.

Connection

Build friendships across generations and create meaningful connections throughout British Columbia.

Community

Create a welcoming space for families, newcomers, students and everyone who wants to stay connected with Assam.
