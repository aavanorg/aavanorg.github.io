WHO WE ARE

01

A community shaped by where we come from — and where we are now.

Assam Association Vancouver brings people together through cultural celebrations, community gatherings, music, dance, food and the traditions that connect us to Assam.

Our goal is simple: create a welcoming place where families, students, newcomers and long-time residents can participate, contribute and keep our culture alive for the next generation.
